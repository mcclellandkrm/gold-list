The Sleeper List

Hardware from 2022–2024 that is still near the top of its class and now sells for a fraction of launch price, not because it aged badly but because it was sold badly.

Read this differently from the Gold List. That list works on demand that already exists: you buy under market and a queue of buyers clears your stock in days.

This list is the opposite trade. These are cheap because nobody is searching for them, and that same silence makes them slow to sell on. Buy these to own, or to put to work. Flip only the few marked for it.

Nothing matches that filter

On the prices. The "new" figure is UK launch RRP. The "now" figure is a working estimate of a fair private-sale price, not a quote. Hit Sold prices on any entry before you offer: that shows what buyers actually paid, where live listings only show what sellers are hoping for.

On the verdicts. Keep means the value is in owning it and resale will be slow. Flip means there is enough residual demand to move it on inside a fortnight. Business kit means it earns rather than sits. Trap means the discount is real but so is the reason for it. Buy to fix means the value only appears after a repair.

Battery Swap. A third kind of discount: good hardware written off because the battery faded. On these entries the "now" figure is a tired or dead unit, and the panel shows battery cost, fixed resale value and the margin between the two. Margin assumes your time is free and the cell is good, so buy batteries from sellers who accept returns. Any sealed device that has been opened is no longer reliably waterproof. Sell it as splash-resistant and say so in the listing.

Two different discounts. Most of this list is cheap because it was sold badly. The Drawer category is cheap for a different reason: high abandonment. Kit bought on enthusiasm for a project that never started, used twice, shelved. It comes to market boxed and barely touched, from a seller who only wants the space back. Worse odds of finding one, far better condition when you do, and the lowest-risk buy on the whole page.

The test that matters. A steep discount tells you nothing on its own. Ask whether the thing was ignored or rejected. Ignored means good hardware that nobody was told about, and the discount is free money. Rejected means buyers tried it and said no, and the discount is the market being right. The Galaxy S25 Edge is on this list purely to hold that line in place.
