# Golden List — Smart Watches (v2)

*April 2026, UK used market. Works alongside the Golden List Framework.*

---

## 0. How to Read This

Framework-first. Order: spec ladder → tier map → true sleepers → secondary → excluded → decoder → checklist. Each entry carries a **"sleeper for whom"** note because serviceability skill (someone with a T80 and heat gun vs a general buyer) changes the call.

---

## 1. The Ladder — Five Axes

Every Golden List call sits somewhere on each:

1. **GPS chip** — single-frequency L1 / dual-frequency L1+L5 (multi-band). Multi-band = chip-ladder equivalent. Fenix 7+, FR 255+, FR 955+, Epix 2+.
2. **HRM generation** — Elevate v3 / v4 / v5. v4 is the floor for real metrics (HRV overnight, sleep stages).
3. **Display** — MIP transflective vs AMOLED. Fork, not hierarchy. MIP = week-long battery + sun readability. AMOLED = smartwatch feel.
4. **Maps** — none / breadcrumb / TopoActive vector. Biggest single price discontinuity in Garmin.
5. **Training stack** — basic → Firstbeat core (VO2, Training Effect) → full Firstbeat (Training Readiness, Stamina, Load Focus, Running Dynamics).

**Sleeper test:** floor high on 3+ axes, priced like it's high on 1.

## 3. True Sleepers (the hunt list)

### Fenix 7 — prefer Pro AMOLED (2024 refresh)

- **Why:** Multi-band GPS, full maps, v4 HRM, full Firstbeat. Fenix 8 (2024) has dropped used prices ~40%. Was "fairly priced" in 2024, is now a sleeper.
- **Hone-in:** £280 (base 7S) → £380 (Pro AMOLED). Walk at +£40.
- **Decoder:** 7S (42mm) / 7 (47mm) / 7X (51mm) × Solar / Sapphire Solar / Pro / Pro Sapphire / Pro Solar / **Pro AMOLED**. Standard 7 has maps (unlike base Fenix 6).
- **Sleeper for whom:** general buyer.

### Epix Gen 2

- **Why:** AMOLED, maps, multi-band, full Firstbeat. Internally close to Fenix 7 Pro AMOLED. Market confuses it with Epix Pro and overpays for the Pro.
- **Hone-in:** £280–340.
- **Decoder:** Gen 2 (42/47mm) vs Gen 2 Pro (bigger chassis, flashlight, better HRM). Don't pay Pro prices for Gen 2.
- **Sleeper for whom:** general buyer, AMOLED preference.

### Forerunner 255 — prefer Music variant

- **Why:** FR 265 (AMOLED 255) absorbs the hype; leaves 255 mispriced. Same training stack. MIP = week-long battery.
- **Hone-in:** £140 base / £165 Music.
- **Decoder:** 255S (41mm) / 255 (46mm) / 255 Music / 255S Music. Music = onboard storage + offline Spotify, worth £25–40 extra, often priced the same.
- **Sleeper for whom:** general buyer, runners especially.

### Garmin Instinct 2X Solar — *specifically 2X Solar*

- **Why:** Floor high on durability + battery (40-day) + full-size GPS antenna. Flashlight is rare at this price. **Base Instinct 2 is fairly priced — don't buy that one thinking you're getting 2X value.**
- **Hone-in:** £180–220.
- **Decoder:** 2S / 2 / 2 Solar / 2X Solar / Tactical. Only 2X Solar = flashlight + full-size chip. Tactical adds stealth mode, usually over-priced.
- **Sleeper for whom:** outdoor use, multi-week battery preference.

### Venu 2 Plus — *note the Plus*

- **Why:** Speaker + mic for calls relayed through phone, v4 HRM, AMOLED. Market doesn't know Plus exists, prices it like base Venu 2.
- **Hone-in:** £140.
- **Decoder:** Venu 2S (40mm) / Venu 2 (45mm) / **Venu 2 Plus** (43mm + speaker/mic).
- **Sleeper for whom:** smartwatch feel, plus anyone with battery-replacement kit.

### Pixel Watch 1

- **Why:** 2022 launch, dismissed for battery complaints. Google extended software support past the panic. Wear OS + Fitbit health stack. Genuine sleeper in the non-Apple/non-Garmin space.
- **Hone-in:** £80–100.
- **Decoder:** Wi-Fi only vs LTE. Prefer Wi-Fi — LTE activation on used is messy.
- **Sleeper for whom:** Pixel phone owner especially; anyone wanting a non-Garmin/non-Apple smartwatch.

## 4. Secondary Sleepers (conditional on price)

### Venu 2 / 2S (base)

- **General buyer:** Secondary. Battery replacement economics kill the value (Garmin official service ~£100, DIY risks water resistance).
- **With T80 + heat gun + 3M 9448A adhesive:** **True Sleeper.** Serviceability Move 2 overrule.
- **Hone-in:** £90 (2S) / £110 (2). Self-service factors ~£25 parts cost; general buyer factors £100 service.

### Apple Watch SE 2 (2022)

- Good at the right price, but Apple drops watchOS support on a rolling ~4-year clock. Check current watchOS compatibility list before buying — SE 2 is probably safe through 2027 but verify.
- **Hone-in:** £130.

### Fenix 6 (base and Pro Solar)

- Becoming what Fenix 5 Plus was last year. Battery and chipset aging together.
- **Hone-in:** £160 base / £240 Pro Solar. Factor battery replacement into total cost.

## 5. Excluded (and why)

- **Fenix 8, Epix Pro Gen 2, FR 965, Watch 10, Ultra 2, Galaxy 7, Pixel Watch 3** — current gen, fairly priced, no gap.
- **Fenix 5 Plus** — 2018 launch, battery cooked on nearly every unit, chipset two gens behind multi-band. Refurb project, not a sleeper.
- **Apple Watch Series 3/4/5** — dropped from current watchOS. Expensive bricks in waiting.
- **Venu Sq / FR 55 / Vivosmart 5** — low-tier. Cheap because cheap. No hidden value.
- **Galaxy Watch 4 base (non-Classic)** — Samsung software support fading. Watch 4 Classic (rotating bezel) was borderline last year, now also drifting toward Excluded.
- **Instinct 2 base** — priced correctly as a ruggedised mid-ranger. The sleeper is specifically the 2X Solar variant.

## 7. Pre-Purchase Checklist — smartwatch-specific

- **Battery behaviour proxy.** Garmin hides battery health. Ask for screenshot at 100% after full charge, and another after 24h normal use. The delta is your diagnostic.
- **Firmware version.** Must be current. Three versions back = sat unused = calendar-aged battery.
- **Account reset.** Factory reset + removed from previous owner's account (Garmin Connect / Apple ID / Google). Not done = features gimped or unit bricked to you.
- **HRM sensor glass.** Scratched = wandering HR readings. Not cosmetic.
- **Strap lug / quick-release pins.** Worn pins = strap flops. Discount lever.
- **AMOLED pixel test.** Full-screen black watchface + full-screen white. Burn-in rare on Garmin AMOLED but not zero on Venu 2 Plus.
- **MIP polariser.** Rainbowing in sunlight = polariser cooked. Not consumer-repairable.
- **Water resistance claim.** Any previously-opened watch (battery service, etc.) has broken IP seal unless professionally re-sealed with fresh gasket. Price accordingly. This is the big one for DIY refurbs.

## 8. Platform Notes

Defer to framework doc. Smartwatch-specific additions:

- **Account lock** is the smartwatch equivalent of iCloud lock. Garmin Connect, Apple ID, Samsung account, Google account — all can gate a used unit. Always verify removal before payment.
- **Vinted** — best for Garmin under £200. Lots of accidental sleepers listed by people who inherited a watch.
- **eBay** — best for Fenix/Epix over £200 (MBG matters at this price point).
- **Facebook Marketplace / Gumtree** — cash-on-collection only, inspect in person, run the checklist live.

## 9. The One-Line Final Check

Before buying:

1. Model number decoded to exact variant?
2. Hone-in price met, including buyer protection + battery/refurb cost?
3. Battery behaviour evidence collected?
4. Account reset confirmed?
5. Am I buying the watch, or the Garmin T-shirt?

Any "no" → pause.

## Appendix — Service Notes

- **Venu 2 / 2S DIY battery service:** batteries plus 3M 9448A adhesive gasket replacement. Craft heat gun on low setting, plastic picks, IPA for adhesive cleanup. Water resistance downgraded from IP rating to "splash-resistant" post-service unless the seal is tested — treat as daily wear only, no swimming.
- **Next category suggestion:** cordless power tool platforms. The framework applies cleanly (platform lock-in = ecosystem, brushless = chip tier, batteries as consumable).
